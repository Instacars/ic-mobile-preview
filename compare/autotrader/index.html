Car shopping should start with what matters to you.

You might need an SUV for a growing family, an efficient commuter for a long drive to work, or something fun for weekend adventures. You might know your budget but have no idea which models to consider.

That's where a different starting point can help.

With Kaia, you can begin with your needs rather than a make and model. Explore different possibilities, ask follow-up questions, and narrow your options as you discover what matters most.

Start with a conversation

You don't need to know the right filters or automotive terminology. Tell Kaia about your lifestyle, budget, priorities, or questions, and start exploring.

Discover possibilities you hadn't considered

Sometimes the right car isn't the one you initially had in mind. Use the conversation to explore alternatives and understand which vehicles could suit your needs.

Compare with more context

Go beyond choosing a vehicle based on its photos or price alone. Explore the features and trade-offs that matter to your decision.

Find your next car with Kaia

AutoTrader is built for browsing. Instacars brings conversation into discovery.

AutoTrader is an established automotive marketplace with a broad selection of listings, search filters, and vehicle research resources. It's useful when you already have an idea of what you want and are ready to narrow down the options.

Instacars approaches the experience from a different starting point: the person behind the search.

Instead of making you figure everything out before you begin, Kaia gives you a conversational way to explore what you need, understand your options, and work toward finding a suitable vehicle.

It's not about replacing every way people shop for cars. It's about offering a more intuitive way to begin.

Which one is right for you?

Choose AutoTrader if…

  • You want to browse a broad selection of vehicle listings.
  • You already know the makes or models you're interested in.
  • You prefer traditional filters and structured vehicle research.
  • You want to explore listings from dealerships and private sellers across Canada.

Try Instacars if...

  • You're not sure which car is right for you yet.
  • You want to start with your lifestyle, budget, or everyday needs.
  • You prefer asking questions and exploring options through conversation.
  • You want help narrowing down possibilities before deciding what to pursue.

Frequently asked questions

Is Instacars an alternative to AutoTrader?
Instacars offers a different approach to car discovery. AutoTrader is an automotive marketplace built around vehicle listings, search, and research. Instacars uses conversational AI to help people explore vehicles and figure out what fits their needs.
Is Instacars better than AutoTrader?
It depends on what you need. AutoTrader is a strong option for browsing a broad inventory and using established search and research tools. Instacars is designed for people who want a more conversational way to discover and evaluate their options. Neither approach is automatically better for every shopper.
Can I compare cars with Instacars?
Kaia is designed to help you explore and compare vehicle options through conversation, so you can focus on the features and considerations that matter to you.
Do I need to know which car I want before using Instacars?
No. The idea is to start with what you know, whether that's your budget, lifestyle, or a few things you'd like in your next car, and explore from there.
Does Instacars sell cars directly?
Instacars focuses on helping shoppers discover and explore vehicles. The next steps depend on the vehicle and the available listing or dealership experience.
