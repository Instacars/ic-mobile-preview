How it works

Finding your next car starts with a conversation.

Forget endless filters and comparing hundreds of listings. Tell Kaia what matters to you, explore your options, and discover cars that fit your life.

Chat with Kaia

01Start with what matters to you

You don't need to know which car you want.

Tell Kaia what you're looking for in your own words. It could be a budget, a lifestyle, a feature you can't live without, or simply a feeling you're after.

Looking for a reliable SUV under $30,000? Need something good on gas for your daily commute? Shopping for your first car? Just start talking.

Try asking:

  • What's a good SUV for a family of five?
  • I need a reliable first car under $20k.
  • I want something fun to drive but practical.

02Discover cars you might love

Find options you wouldn't have thought to search for.

Kaia helps you explore vehicles that match your needs, preferences, and budget. You'll see relevant options and understand what makes each one worth considering.

Instead of sorting through endless listings, spend your time discovering cars that actually make sense for you.

Find my car

03Keep exploring with Kaia

Every question takes you somewhere new.

Not sure whether you need an SUV or a sedan? Wondering if hybrid is worth it? Want to know which cars offer Apple CarPlay or enough room for your gear?

Ask follow-up questions, explore suggested ideas, and uncover details you might not have considered. Kaia helps you build on what you've learned, so you can discover better options as you go.

Your conversation could lead to:

  • Better options for your budget
  • Features you didn't know you needed
  • Alternatives worth considering
  • New ways to narrow down your search

04Compare what matters

Go beyond the badge.

Once you've found a few interesting options, explore what sets them apart. Look at the features, practicality, mileage, and details that matter to your decision.

Kaia helps you make sense of your options so you can decide which vehicles deserve a closer look.

05Take the next step

From curious to ready for a test drive.

Found something interesting? Explore the available vehicle details, check out listings from dealers, and move toward booking a test drive when you're ready.

No pressure to decide immediately. The goal is to help you feel more confident about what comes next.

Start discovering cars

Your next car could be one question away.

You bring the questions. Kaia helps you explore the possibilities.

Start with what you know, discover what you didn't, and find a car that fits your life.
