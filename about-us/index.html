About Us

We're rethinking how people find their next car.

We're bringing a new perspective to the automotive industry, connecting the people looking for their next car with the businesses helping them find it. Different sides of the same journey, united by one belief: finding the right car should be easier for everyone.

Try Kaia

The mission

Making car discovery more human.

Instacars is rethinking how people navigate the journey of finding a car. We believe it should be easier to explore possibilities, understand your options, and make confident decisions. We're building a future where technology brings people and the automotive world closer together.

What we believe

Four beliefs guide everything we build.

  • 01: Technology should feel human. Make complicated things simpler, not more complicated.
  • 02. Discovery goes beyond search. Help people uncover possibilities they didn't know to look for.
  • 03. Trust comes before transactions. Better decisions start with useful information and transparency.
  • 04. Better experiences benefit everyone. The automotive journey can work better for both the people buying cars and the people selling them.

How we work

Build with purpose. Learn from reality.

We challenge assumptions, listen to the people we're building for, and focus on solving real problems rather than adding technology for its own sake. We believe the best products come from understanding people's needs, testing ideas in the real world, and continuously improving what we create.

Work with us

Help us rethink car discovery.

We're building something different for an industry ready for a better experience. Whether you're exploring Instacars as a customer, interested in working with us, or looking to collaborate, we'd love to hear from you.
